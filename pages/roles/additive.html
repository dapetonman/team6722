ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING LEAD

LORENZO MORI

Additive manufacturing is a type of manufacturing technique that works by adding material to a surface, i.e, 3D printing, resin printing. We use 3D printing for custom plastic parts that can't be made by subtractive manufacturing. Additive manufacturing at LED Robotics is usually used for small parts, prototypes, or objects that don't need as much strength as metal.
